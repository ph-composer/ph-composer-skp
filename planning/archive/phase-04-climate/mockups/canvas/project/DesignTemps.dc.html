Design temperatures

The ASHRAE design dry-bulb temperatures for the site. A Phius station file carries neither. Take them from the ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals climatic design conditions for the nearest station.

Used for the blended heat pump on the Mechanical tab. Export does not wait on them: without both, only the blended heat pump is left out, with a finding. Clear a field to unset it.
