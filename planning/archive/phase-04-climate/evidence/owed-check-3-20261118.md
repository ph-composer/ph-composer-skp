The corpus is the one `_private/climate/MANIFEST.md` locates, read in place.

## Host

SketchUp 26.1.188, Ruby 3.2.2, macOS, a fresh process launched by `host_bridge.py launch` on a
fresh copy of `_private/host/blank-template.skp`, branch `feature/climate-p4c-04` at its base
`5f307da3` installed with `make rbz install bridge-install`. The read ran in a probe script loaded
through `host_bridge.py eval`, which needs no window focus; it walked the folder, read each
`-mon.txt` file's bytes, transcoded them from Windows-1252 and found the header row, and answered
counts and seconds only.

## 2. A cloud-synced folder whose files are not downloaded

Not run. The corpus sits in `~/Dropbox`, a plain folder kept by Dropbox's own sync client, with no
file in a not-downloaded state (`find -flags dataless` counted 0 of 2,166). Making one would mean
setting files to online-only in Ed's Dropbox, a change to his sync settings that this stage may not
make. Nothing was evicted and no sync state changed.

The design does not depend on it: every read is bounded (below), so a folder of not-downloaded files
costs at most one blocked read per station file the walk opens, each of at most 64 KiB. Whether a
read of such a file blocks, downloads or fails is still owed, with the CM04 host clause.

## 3. D4c-11

Under 0.5 s, so the read is synchronous: no `DeferredWork` slicing and no cache (a warm read is far
under 2 s). The plan's split rule does not apply; P4c-04 is one stage. The walk is bounded instead:
20,000 entries, 16 folders deep, files of at most 64 KiB, links not followed, hidden names skipped;
past a bound it stops and reports `complete: false`.

## 4. C4c-6

`Digest::SHA256` loads in SketchUp 2026's Ruby 3.2.2 (`require "digest"`), and its digest of `abc`
begins `ba7816bf`, the published test vector.

## 5. C4c-2

The matching name is the file name's stem, with the state folder. In the corpus no two files in a
state folder share a stem (0 repeats), while 3 header names repeat within their state folder, and 3
of 1,007 header names differ from their file's stem after normalising case and punctuation. The
record keeps the header's name as `name`, and `provenance.station_name` is the stem.

## 6. The format, from structure only

Read against PH-Navigator's Phius importer
and counted over the corpus without printing a value:

- 1,007 `-mon.txt` files in 50 state folders; every one is CRLF and holds bytes above 127 (the degree
  sign in its labels), with no byte-order mark. No cell uses a comma as a decimal separator.
- Line 1 is blank and line 2 is the header row in every file. 1,005 files have 17 lines; 1 repeats
  its whole block (the first occurrence wins); 1 has extra notes after the block.
- Header row: the name, then `Latitude [°]`, `Longitude [°]`, `Height a.s.l. (m)` each followed by
  its value, two empty cells, `Daily temperature variation summer (K)` and its value, two cells, and
  the three design-column labels. 1 file holds other labels in the two spare cell pairs and a fourth
  design label; 1 has no design labels. So the reader takes each label's next non-empty cell and
  does not rely on the design labels.
- Series rows, lines 3 to 10 in this order: `Temperature outdoor`, `North`, `East`, `South`, `West`,
  `Global`, `Dewpoint`, `Sky temperature`. Every one has exactly 15 non-empty cells after its label.
  All twelve months are plain decimals in every file. The design cells are three numbers for the
  temperature and five radiation rows, and three text notes for `Dewpoint` and `Sky temperature`.
- Line 11: `Heating degree-hours (12/20)`, a value, `Cooling degree-hours (24)`, a value, `Wind
  speed`, `Jan`, a value, `Jul`, a value, `m/s`, in every file.
- After it: a German-labelled row whose values carry `°C` and `%`, two statistics notes and
  `Albedo = ` with a value.
- Beside the stations, the tree holds 5 other files whose names do not end `-mon.txt` and one hidden
  system file.

The product reader over the corpus: 1,007 stations in 50 states, all 1,007 parsing whole; the five
other files counted as not read (4 `.txt`, 1 `.txt(2)`); over the whole corpus folder, 1,149 `.xls`
more. `tests/climate_station_corpus_test.rb`, when `PH_COMPOSER_CLIMATE_CORPUS` is set, asserts that
the walk is complete, that every listed station parses whole, and that every file is either a station
or counted as not read; it does not hold the counts above, which would put the corpus's shape in a
public test. It skips when the variable is unset.
