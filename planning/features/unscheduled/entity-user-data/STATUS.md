## Blockers and open decisions

None. Ed answered D-UD-1 to D-UD-8 as recommended on 2026-11-14.

Follow-ups from EUD-01, closed in EUD-08: the foundation example's `update_parts` no longer takes
`user_data`; its walkthrough writes the map through `records.update_metadata`, and
`Engine#propose_update` no longer takes `user_data:` (no caller remained). The `request.malformed`
and `params.invalid` split is documented in `context/CONTRACTS.md` and `docs/USER_DATA.md`.

Follow-up from EUD-07, closed in EUD-08: an `export.metadata_omitted` finding on a Layer,
Layer-Segment or Window Element now carries `reference`, its record's kind and id with the part's id
path, and names the record in its message.

Follow-up from EUD-05, closed in EUD-06: writing `{}` through an entity owner's `update_metadata` to a
legacy record stored without the maps committed a step that added them, where `records.update_metadata`
wrote nothing. `Records::Metadata.updated` now leaves an absent map absent when it is cleared, so every
owner, the catalog action and the new Model and Space Floor writes treat it as no change.
