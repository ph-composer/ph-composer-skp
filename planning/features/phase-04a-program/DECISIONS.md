| F4a-01 | Nothing in PH-Composer writes a Program, load or schedule. Rooms fall back to Plenum and PHX's WUFI conversion fails without a People load. | F4a-03 | Program loads are normalised (per m² floor, per person, per m² exterior area, ACH). A per-Zone absolute value is a Room-level override whose per-area value depends on that Room's exported floor or exposed area. A Room with no floor face silently keeps a zero load | [programs](research/honeybee-energy-programs.md#2-room-inheritance-and-overrides) |
| F4a-04 | A program shared by many Rooms is locked on assignment; editing means duplicate and reassign. This maps onto PH-Composer's definition record plus Zone assignment, with overrides as a separate per-Zone record | [programs](research/honeybee-energy-programs.md#1-programtype) |
| F4a-05 | Schedules are shared by identifier across loads and programs and emitted once at Model level. Loading has no library fallback: every schedule and type limit named must be in the file. | F4a-06 | honeybee-energy never checks schedule values against their type limit, and Python and honeybee-schema disagree on several ranges. PH-Composer must validate values itself, to the stricter rule | [loads and schedules](research/loads-and-schedules.md#0-rules-an-exporter-must-follow) |
| F4a-07 | Residential occupants, bedrooms and dwellings live on `People.properties.ph`, not on the Room or building segment. PHX sums occupants and bedrooms per segment and counts units by dwelling identifier. Stage P4-12's "where are the per-Zone dwelling and bedroom counts stored" is answered by this packet | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#people-and-occupancy) |
| F4a-08 | PHX forks on the building segment's Phius `building_category_type`: residential reads PhEquipment and ignores ElectricEquipment and Process watts; non-residential does the reverse. | F4a-09 | The Infiltration load is the only airtightness path to WUFI, METr and PHPP. It stores m³/s·m² of exposed area at 4 Pa; PHX converts to q50 with exponent 0.65 and to n50 with net volume. Authoring n50 or q50 means the exporter inverts that conversion | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#infiltration-and-airtightness) |
| F4a-10 | The Program's Ventilation load is the Zone default that Spaces already say they inherit. PHX sums its four terms (ignoring `method`) and splits by Space floor share; any Space `_v_*` value overrides it | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#ventilation), [current state](research/ph-composer-current-state.md#2-zones-spaces-and-the-building-segment) |
| F4a-11 | Setpoints exist twice: the Program's Setpoint load (EnergyPlus only; PHX ignores it) and the building segment's `set_points` (PHX reads it). Hot water exists twice: the ServiceHotWater load (EnergyPlus only; PHX never reads it) and the honeybee-ph hot water system (Phase 4 equipment) | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#service-hot-water) |
| F4a-12 | Schedules have two representations. honeybee-energy rulesets (day profiles, weekday rules, date ranges) are what EnergyPlus reads. honeybee-ph operating periods (`properties.ph`) are what PHX prefers for WUFI utilization patterns; without them PHX derives patterns from the hourly values. PHX's ventilation branch requires the period names high, standard, basic and minimum | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#3-schedules-into-utilization-patterns) |
| F4a-13 | On the operating-period path PHX counts the daily window twice for occupancy and lighting: a Phius meeting room comes out at a relative factor of 0.229 instead of 0.5, and 1,260 lighting full-load hours instead of 2,750. The rulesets-only path is unaffected. **Confirmed by a run** on 2026-11-14 (PHX 1.56.104, honeybee-ph 1.33.68, honeybee-energy 1.117.10 in the PHX venv): occupancy factor 0.2292 (WUFI RelativeAbsenteeism 0.7708), lighting 1,260.4 full-load hours against 2,750 operating hours. The same property also skews EnergyPlus: honeybee-ph's Phius builder writes it as a constant over all 8,760 h, a fraction sum of 2,007.5 against the intended 1,375 (+46 %). WUFI's relative absence applies inside the utilization window (DIN V 18599-10, cited by PHX at `to_WUFI_xml/xml_schemas.py:1840`), so `annual_average_operating_fraction` serves neither consumer | [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#3-schedules-into-utilization-patterns) |
| F4a-14 | PH-Composer ships only honeybee-energy's two default programs. honeybee-ph's 44 Phius programs all build on honeybee-energy-standards `2019::` base programs, which are not shipped (about 12 MB with schedules), so they cannot be built in the export runtime as pinned | [programs](research/honeybee-energy-programs.md#4-where-programs-come-from), [PH stack](research/ph-stack-consumption.md#4-where-ph-program-defaults-live) |
| F4a-15 | ScheduleFixedInterval has no `properties.ph` and PHX reads that block unguarded on People, Lighting and Ventilation schedules. Schedules PHX reads must be ScheduleRuleset | [loads and schedules](research/loads-and-schedules.md#schedulefixedinterval-schedulefixedintervalpy) |
| F4a-16 | The local honeybee-energy checkout adds ExhaustAir; the pinned 1.123.23 does not. Exhaust is out of scope until the pin moves | [programs](research/honeybee-energy-programs.md#baseline) |
| F4a-17 | honeybee-ph has no bare Process load. Every Process carries a `PhEquipment` device on `properties.ph.ph_equipment` (appliances, Phius MEL, Phius interior, exterior and garage lighting, custom annual kWh, elevators). Its watts are `annual_avg_wattage`: annual kWh from occupants, bedrooms, dwellings and floor area, divided by the schedule's value sum. The Grasshopper components add these Processes to Rooms, never to a ProgramType. PHX residential merges them across Rooms by device identifier and sums `quantity` (`from_HBJSON/cleanup.py`, `merge_process_loads`); `PhEquipment.duplicate()` keeps the identifier on purpose for that merge. PHX non-residential drops the device and exports `watts × full-load hours` per Room. Residential interior lighting rides on the Lighting load instead (`Lighting.properties.ph.ph_equipment`). `hvac/supportive_device.py` is dead: it raises on construction and points to `honeybee_phhvac` | `honeybee_energy_ph/load/ph_equipment.py`, `properties/load/process.py`; `honeybee_grasshopper_ph` `program/add_process_equip.py`, `create_elec_equip.py`, `set_res_program.py` |
| F4a-18 | `load/ph_equipment.py` differs between the pinned honeybee-ph 1.33.65 and the local 1.33.68: `quantity` defaults to 0 in the pin and 1 locally, the custom annual types multiply by `quantity` only locally, and the pin still has `PhEquipmentCollection`. PHX reads a stored 0 as 1. The pin must be chosen before PH-Composer writes `PhEquipment` dicts | diff of the pinned wheel against the local checkout |
| F4a-19 | honeybee-ph occupancy: a dwelling is a `PhDwellings` object (identifier, `num_dwellings`) on each Room's `People.properties.ph`. Rooms that form one unit share the object; one Room can hold N units. A Room with `num_dwellings < 1` is untagged and groups alone. `number_bedrooms` and `number_people` are set per Room. Set Res Occupancy derives `people_per_area` per dwelling group: Σ occupants ÷ occupancy-schedule mean ÷ group floor area. Dwelling identity never goes on `Room.zone`, which honeybee-energy reads as an EnergyPlus zone merge. PHX counts units over unique dwelling identifiers | `honeybee_energy_ph/dwellings.py`, `properties/load/people.py`; `honeybee_grasshopper_ph` `program/set_dwelling.py`, `set_res_occupancy.py`, `get_res_occupancy.py` |
| F4a-20 | honeybee-ph's Set Residential Program builds a whole residential program per dwelling group: People from occupants over gross floor area; Lighting from the Phius RESNET interior formula over net Space-weighted area, carrying a `PhPhiusLightingInterior`; ElectricEquipment at 0 W/m² (all MEL through Process devices); Infiltration 0.0003 m³/s·m²; Ventilation 0.4 ACH; Setpoint from the SFH heating and cooling schedules with 60 % dehumidification; hot water from Building America Table 11 by bedrooms; plus the eight-device Phius default set as Processes. Its schedules (`hbph_sfh_*.json`: occupant presence and activity, lighting, MEL, five appliances, combined hot water, heating and cooling setpoints) and `default_elec_equip` ship in the pinned honeybee-ph wheel. The shower term in its code is `1.17 × Nbr`; its docstring and the cited table say `4.67 × Nbr` | `honeybee_grasshopper_ph` `program/set_res_program.py`; pinned `honeybee_ph_standards/schedules/`, `programtypes/default_elec_equip.py` |
| F4a-21 | REVIVE uses the same shape: a template program and schedules loaded by name from its standards package, then loads set from building totals. Its Phius occupancy rule is `(bedrooms ÷ dwellings + 1) × dwellings`. Its residential program sets `watts_per_area` to annual kWh ÷ iCFA with no hours term; check the units before reusing that math | `honeybee_REVIVE_grasshopper` `standards/create_revive_residential_program.py`, `resiliency/set_resiliency_program.py` |
| F4a-22 | A Phius Multifamily common-area Program that carries electric equipment (Common Office 42.3 W/m², Workout Room 12.04 W/m²) loses that load in a residential Building Segment: PHX's residential path reads only `PhEquipment` devices and skips the Program's ElectricEquipment and Process watts | PHX `from_HBJSON/create_variant.py` (`add_elec_equip_from_hb_room`), `create_process_loads.py` (`is_phius_non_residential`); values from honeybee-ph `PHIUS_programs.py` |
| F4a-23 | The Phius program data carries only people, lighting and electric equipment, each with its schedule. Ventilation, infiltration, hot water and setpoints come from the `2019::` base programs, so a library file built without those bases has no values for them | honeybee-ph `PHIUS_programs.py`, `library/programtypes.py` (`build_hb_program_from_Phius_data`) |
