## Deviation: feature packets are grouped by roadmap phase

Ed asked for this on 2026-09-30. The canon's `features/<slug>/` becomes
`features/<group>/<slug>/`, where the group is one of:

- `phase-NN-<name>/`, one per roadmap phase that still has open packets. The directory of the
  phase being worked also holds that phase's coordinating packet: `README.md`, `PLAN.md`,
  `CONTRACTS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `ACCEPTANCE.md`, `STATUS.md`, `phases/` and later `evidence/`.
  A phase not yet planned in detail has a `README.md` router only.
- `phase-NNx-<name>/`, a lettered sub-phase, when a roadmap phase holds features independent
  enough to plan and work one at a time. Ed asked for this on 2026-10-03 and split Phase 3 into
  `phase-03a-spaces/` and `phase-03b-thermal-bridges/`. A sub-phase with one feature is one flat
  packet: the feature's `PRD.md` beside the coordinating files, with one `README.md` and one
  `STATUS.md` that is its tracker. Ids restart per sub-phase and carry its letter: stages
  `P3a-01`, decisions `D3a-1`, stage documents `phases/p<phase><letter>-<nn>-<short-title>.md`.
  The roadmap phase closes when all its sub-phases close; each is archived under its own slug.
- `cross-phase/` for packets delivered in slices across several phases.
- `unscheduled/` for packets with no roadmap phase.

A packet lives where its remaining work is. When the roadmap moves a packet, move its folder with
`git mv` and fix the relative links in the same change. The archive is unchanged: flat by slug,
`archive/<slug>/`, with the phase packet and each feature packet archived under their own slugs.
Stage documents in a phase packet are named `phases/p<phase>-<nn>-<short-title>.md`, or
`phases/p<phase><letter>-<nn>-<short-title>.md` in a sub-phase.

Ed inserted a post-Spaces agent-access phase on 2026-10-17 using the exact folder name
`features/phase-03a1/` (archived 2026-10-18 at `archive/phase-03a1/`). This explicit naming exception uses `P3a1-01` stage IDs,
`D3a1-01` decision IDs and `phases/p3a1-01-<short-title>.md` stage files. Its coordinating packet
owns one status ledger; `workstreams/` groups the detailed stages by logical owner without adding
competing progress ledgers. Planning authorization for this insertion excludes implementation.

## Quick reference (from the canon)

1. **Plans are provisional; `context/` is canonical.** When a plan lands,
   fold the outcome into the relevant `context/` doc.
2. **`STATUS.md` is the index.** Every active item gets a row with its
   GitHub issue link.
3. **One folder (or file) per unit of work**, lowercase kebab-case, inside its phase group.
4. **Issue-first intake**; packets link their issue; PRs say `Closes #N`.
5. **Archive flat by slug** to `archive/<slug>/` + one row in
   `archive/README.md`. Never nested by date.
6. **Public repo: no client names, BT numbers, or licensed data** in issues
   or planning docs.
